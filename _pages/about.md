I'm an eFX quantitative trader based in Madrid, working on algorithmic pricing, market making, and liquidity optimization in spot FX markets.

My interests span market microstructure, optimal execution, inventory management, adverse selection, and the intersection of machine learning with electronic trading.

This blog is a collection of practitioner notes — things I've learned, frameworks I've found useful, and ideas worth writing down. Occasionally I'll share open source tools.
